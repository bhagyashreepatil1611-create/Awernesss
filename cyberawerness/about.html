The human side of security

Awareness is your first line of defense.

Cyber awareness means understanding the online risks around you and making informed choices to protect your information, devices, and relationships.

Why it matters

Digital safety is an everyday skill.

We study, work, shop, and stay in touch online. A little knowledge helps people recognize pressure tactics and respond before a small mistake becomes a bigger problem.

01 / MEANING

What is cyber awareness?

It is the knowledge and judgement to spot possible online threats, protect personal data, and choose a safe next step.

02 / IMPORTANCE

Why does it matter?

Scams and account abuse can affect privacy, finances, study, and peace of mind. Awareness helps reduce exposure and recover sooner.

03 / RISKS

Common digital risks

Phishing, harmful downloads, password reuse, impersonation, privacy leaks, and misleading websites all deserve a second look.

04 / PROTECTION

How awareness protects

Pausing to verify a request, using unique passwords, enabling extra sign-in checks, and updating software can interrupt common attacks.

05 / COMMUNITY

Students and citizens

Students can question suspicious messages and share trusted advice. Every citizen can report abuse, respect privacy, and help others find reliable support.

06 / RESPONSE

Respond without blame

If something goes wrong, act quickly: secure affected accounts, contact the real service through official channels, and ask a trusted person for help.
