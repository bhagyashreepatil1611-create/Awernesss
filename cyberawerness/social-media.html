Share with care

Your social life. Your boundaries.

Every platform has different controls, but the basics stay the same: protect your account, choose your audience, and take a moment before you post.

Platform-aware habits

Stay thoughtful on every feed.

Settings change over time. Use each platform's current privacy and security menus to review who can contact you and see what you share.

PHOTO & STORIES

Instagram

Review private-account and story audience settings. Be cautious with direct-message links, impersonation accounts, and requests to share verification codes.

FRIENDS & GROUPS

Facebook

Check post audiences and profile visibility. Ignore unfamiliar friend requests, watch for cloned profiles, and report accounts pretending to be someone you know.

MESSAGING

WhatsApp

Limit who can add you to groups, verify unusual requests from a known contact another way, and never forward a login code or one-time password.

VIDEO & COMMENTS

YouTube

Think about personal details visible in videos or comments. Use account security checks and be wary of fake giveaways or links in comments.

ANY PLATFORM

Everywhere else

Use a unique password and multifactor authentication. Review privacy settings, block or report suspicious accounts, and keep your apps updated.

BEFORE YOU POST

Protect your future self

A post can be copied and reshared. Avoid revealing private information, location, daily routines, or details you would not share with everyone.
