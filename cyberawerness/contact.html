If you think an account or device is compromised

Act quickly. Get trusted help.

01 / SECURE
!

Emergency cyber safety advice

From a trusted device, change the affected password and any reused passwords. Sign out other sessions, turn on multifactor authentication, and contact your bank immediately if payment details may be exposed. Preserve suspicious messages and report them through official channels. If a device may have malware, disconnect it from networks and contact trusted technical support.

02 / CHECK
✓

Quick safety checklist

Never share your password or one-time code. Verify unexpected requests through a known number or official app. Update your devices, keep backups, use unique passwords, and tell a trusted person if someone is pressuring or threatening you online.
